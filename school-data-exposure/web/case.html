Case file (provisional)

Narrative draft pending DI-15. Facts below are limited to Documented public evidence.

Executive summary

CSUSB documents Canvas/Instructure, myCoyote, DUO MFA, Google Analytics, and Canvas learning apps including Turnitin and Respondus webcam proctoring. The website Privacy Notice names analytics and vendor categories but does not give students a named LMS processor inventory. The core exposure is opacity plus high-sensitivity monitoring inside coursework.
