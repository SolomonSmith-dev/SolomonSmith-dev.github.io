# Student self-audit checklist

Use only your own accounts. Stop if a step requires unauthorized access.

## A. Public policy sweep

1. Find the campus privacy / security notice.
2. Find FERPA and student-records policies.
3. Find information-security policy index.
4. Find ITS services directory.
5. Save URL, retrieval date, and hash (use `scripts/ingest_evidence.py`).

## B. Own-account browser audit

1. Log in with your account only.
2. Open one LMS page and one SIS/home portal page.
3. Record third-party domains from Network.
4. Inventory cookies by host.
5. Redact tokens before any share or commit.

## C. Compare claims vs practice

1. Does the privacy notice name the trackers you observed?
2. Are vendors discoverable without digging?
3. Is there a retention statement for learning analytics?
4. Are opt-outs real and reachable?

## D. Escalate lawfully

1. Draft a records request (see `cpra-request-template.md`).
2. Wait for Decision Interview #12 before sending.
3. Track responses; publish only released documents.
