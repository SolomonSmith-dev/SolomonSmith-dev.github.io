# School Data Exposure — Legal Charter (draft v0.1)

**Status:** draft pending Decision Interview #2 (Legal Charter)  
**Provisional target:** California State University, San Bernardino (CSUSB)  
**Owner:** Solomon Smith

## Mission

Document, with citable public evidence and optional own-account observations, how campus systems and EdTech vendors collect, share, and retain student data -- and where policy language diverges from practice.

## Non-negotiables

1. No unauthorized access to school systems.
2. No use of anyone else's credentials.
3. No doxxing of students, staff, or faculty.
4. No exploits, credential stuffing, scraping behind auth walls beyond the author's own session, or CFAA-risk activity.
5. No publishing raw HAR files, cookies, tokens, student IDs, or emails.
6. Every public claim must carry a claim grade: **Alleged**, **Documented**, or **Verified**.
7. Vendors may be named only when tied to a public source or a redacted own-account observation approved in a decision interview.
8. This project is not legal advice.

## Allowed methods

- Public policies, IT pages, vendor docs, news, and court/public records
- California Public Records Act / CPRA-style requests after DI-12 approval
- Browser DevTools observation of the author's own logged-in sessions, with mandatory redaction
- Comparison of policy claims against observed third-party requests

## Claim grades

| Grade | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Alleged | Hypothesis or tip; not publishable as fact |
| Documented | Supported by a retained public source or redacted own-account capture |
| Verified | Documented plus independent corroboration (second source, contract text, or reproducible steps) |

## Correction policy

If the school, a vendor, or a reader challenges a finding with evidence, publish a correction or update within a defined SLA after DI-19. Prefer accuracy over narrative momentum.

## Acceptance

Charter is accepted only when DI-02 status is `locked` by Solomon.
