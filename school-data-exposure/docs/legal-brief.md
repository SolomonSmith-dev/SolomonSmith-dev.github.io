# Legal framing brief (draft v0.1)

**Gate:** DI-09  
**Disclaimer:** Not legal advice.

## Sources students are pointed to

- FERPA campus policy page
- Student Records Administration policy
- Privacy and Security Notice (Sites)

## Observed structural issue

Rights documents exist, but the authenticated EdTech stack (Canvas + learning apps + myCoyote + MFA) is only partially connected to the website Privacy Notice. Students can read FERPA language and still lack a processor list for Turnitin/Respondus/Google Analytics-class tools.

## Student-actionable rights (high level)

1. Inspect education records pathways described in campus FERPA/records policies.
2. Directory information opt-out where offered.
3. Public records requests for contracts/DPAs (after DI-12).
4. Demand course-level notice before webcam proctoring.

## What this project will not do

- Allege criminal violations without Verified evidence
- Provide counsel
- Publish private student identifiers
