# Harm model (draft v0.1)

**Gate:** DI-05 (provisional research draft)

## Abuse vs legitimate use

Legitimate educational use: grading, advising, accessibility, security MFA, required records retention.  
Abuse (operational definition): collection, monitoring, or sharing that is unnecessary for the stated educational purpose, hidden from students, or mismatched to published notices -- especially biometric/webcam surveillance and opaque vendor chains.

## Harm categories

1. **Surveillance chill** -- webcam proctoring and continuous analytics change student behavior.
2. **Opaque processing** -- students cannot name who receives their data.
3. **Scope mismatch** -- website privacy notice does not describe LMS app stack.
4. **Linkage** -- SSO + MFA + portal unify identity across apps.
5. **Retention risk** -- unclear how long proctoring video / integrity reports live.
6. **Secondary use** -- marketing vendors named beside education management processors.

## Severity rubric

| Level | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| S0 | Informational / baseline documentation |
| S1 | Transparency gap with limited sensitivity |
| S2 | Tracking or sharing beyond obvious UX expectation |
| S3 | High-sensitivity monitoring (e.g., webcam) or broad vendor sharing without inventory |
| S4 | Evidence of unlawful or deceptive practice (requires Verified bar; none claimed yet) |

## Non-goals

Not anti-assessment. Not anti-security MFA. Not a call to hack systems.
