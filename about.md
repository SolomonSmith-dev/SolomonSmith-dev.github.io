I'm a Computer Science student at **California State University, San Bernardino**, focused on
artificial intelligence, machine learning, and full-stack engineering. I'm most interested
in the systems side of ML — turning models into reliable, observable applications people
actually use.

Before tech, I spent over a decade in the **culinary industry**. Long shifts, narrow
margins for error, and constant collaboration taught me how to ship under pressure,
keep a system running when something breaks, and refine a process until it's right.
Those habits carried straight into how I write software.
